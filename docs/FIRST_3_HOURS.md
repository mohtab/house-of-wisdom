# First 3 Hours

## Opening targets
- First reward: under 1 minute
- First House restoration: 3 to 5 minutes
- Mathematics unlock: 7 to 10 minutes
- First meaningful choice: about 15 minutes
- Al-Kindi Chronicle: about 30 minutes
- Al-Kindi complete: about 45 minutes
- Offline-return prompt: 50 to 60 minutes
- Astronomy unlock: 90 to 120 minutes elapsed
- Al-Battani teaser: 2 to 3 hours elapsed

## Opening flow
1. Start with Translation and one damaged manuscript.
2. Earn Knowledge and Translation XP.
3. Restore the desk.
4. Unlock Mathematics through research.
5. Let the player choose a Translation or Mathematics boost.
6. Unlock the Al-Kindi Chronicle after sufficient Translation and Mathematics progress.
7. Complete the Chronicle and restore the Scriptorium.
8. Introduce offline progression.
9. Unlock the Observatory and Astronomy.
10. End the slice with the Al-Battani Chronicle discovered but not yet completed.

## Prototype gate
Do not build the rest of Chapter One until this sequence is enjoyable with placeholder visuals.

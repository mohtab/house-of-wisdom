# Historical Guidelines

House of Wisdom uses real historical figures through Chronicles rather than treating them as random collectible characters.

## Rules
- Use credible historical and academic sources before public release.
- Do not imply that scholars from different centuries all lived or worked together.
- Separate historical fact from fictional framing and gameplay invention.
- Avoid trivia-style teaching. Let the player interact with an idea or method.
- Avoid generic Arabian Nights imagery and orientalist stereotypes.
- Design Arabic and English together rather than translating Arabic at the end.
- Use the broader Arabic-language and Islamic scientific ecosystem accurately, without forcing every figure into one ethnicity.

## Current Chronicles
### Al-Kindi
Focus: analytical reasoning and cryptanalysis through patterns and frequency.

### Al-Battani
Focus: repeated astronomical observation and increasingly accurate celestial tables.

### Ibn al-Haytham
Deferred until the first slice is validated. Planned focus: experimentation with light, observation, and competing explanations of vision.

## Contribution standard
Historical corrections are welcome. A content change that makes a factual claim should include or point to a credible source when practical.
